Climate and Mobility

Here, we will discuss one of the application from my own research work. Where we are trying to understand the co-occurence of climate signals and mobility signals. Question: Where and when do mobility signals coincide with high reference evaporative demand or low vegetation cover?

Study period: 2013–2015 · Spatial unit: matched origin/destination zones · Time unit: half-month

Data Zone × period summary
METREF Half-month cumulative ETo and mean daily ETo
FVC Half-month mean FVC and change from the previous period
Mobility Departures, returns or migrants; counts and population-adjusted rates

Maps: climate and mobility side by side; then map co-occurrence classes such as high ETo + low FVC + high departures.
